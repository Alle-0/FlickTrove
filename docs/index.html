FAQ

Everything you need to know about FlickTrove.

Yes, 100% free and source-available on GitHub. There are no advertisements, no tracking, and no paywalled features. You have complete ownership of your data.

Absolutely. FlickTrove supports seamless two-way synchronization with Trakt and SIMKL, as well as universal CSV and JSON backup imports from TV Time, Letterboxd, and other tracking services.

Yes. FlickTrove is built from the ground up with an offline-first architecture. Your watched history, custom folders, lists, and statistics are stored locally on your device with lightning-fast Room database queries.

Metadata, high-resolution posters, trailers, and ratings are aggregated directly from TMDB, TheTVDB, OMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, and Metacritic to provide a comprehensive, cinematic tracking experience.

FlickTrove supports 13 languages natively: English, Italian, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese, Indonesian, and Turkish. It also includes localized regional film classifications (MPAA, Eirin, KMRB, LSF, RTÜK, FSK, CNC, ICAA, ClassInd, CBFC, and BBFC).

FlickTrove lets you read and write reviews, share reactions, and discuss movies and TV shows. Social comments and federated community discussions are integrated with commsuni.tv (CommsUni), keeping discussions vibrant, open, and moderated across platforms.
